What we fit

Seven ways to cover a window properly.

Each one suits a different room and a different problem. If you're not sure which, that's what the free measure is for — we'll tell you honestly which is worth the money in your house.

Plantation shutters

The most substantial option. Real or composite wood louvres built to the exact window, framed and hinged like joinery. They insulate, they never need replacing, and they read as part of the house rather than something hung in front of it.

  • Real wood or moisture-safe composite
  • Best insulation of any covering
  • Adds resale value
  • Bathrooms & kitchens included
Get a price for this

Roller shades

Clean, minimal and the most versatile thing we fit. One roll of fabric, hundreds of choices — from a sheer that keeps the view and kills the glare, to a full blackout that makes a bedroom genuinely dark at 7am in June.

  • Sheer, light-filtering or blackout
  • Hundreds of fabrics
  • Slimmest stack when open
  • Best value per window
Get a price for this

Zebra (dual) shades

Alternating sheer and solid bands that slide over one another. Line them up and you have a sheer; offset them and you have privacy — all from the same blind, without swapping anything. The most-requested covering in new GTA builds, and it's not close.

  • Light control without changing blinds
  • Modern, architectural look
  • Great for street-facing rooms
  • Most popular choice
Get a price for this

Silhouette & sheer shades

Soft fabric vanes floating between two sheers. They turn hard afternoon sun into diffused light and give you privacy without going dark — the reason they end up in front-facing living rooms more than anywhere else.

  • Diffuses harsh direct sun
  • Privacy while staying light
  • UV protection for floors & furniture
  • The premium finish

Motorised blinds

Quiet motors, rechargeable battery or hardwired. Raise a whole room from one remote. No cords anywhere, which is also why they're the right call in any home with young children or pets.

  • Battery or hardwired
  • Whole-room and whole-house remotes
  • Completely cordless — child safe
  • Retrofit to most existing blinds

Smart blinds

Motorised blinds wired into the rest of your home. They lift with sunrise, close at dusk, and drop on their own when the afternoon sun hits the south side. Same app, same voice assistant, same schedule as your lights.

  • Sunrise / sunset schedules
  • Voice and app control
  • Drops automatically on hot afternoons
  • Works with your existing setup

Blinds that behave like part of the house.

Most blinds companies can sell you a motor. Very few can tie that motor into your lighting, your climate and your voice assistant — because that's a different trade. We do both, which is the whole reason we sell blinds at all.

How you can buy

Three ways to work with us on blinds.

Supply & install — the usual route

We measure, help you choose the type and fabric, place the order with our suppliers, and come back to fit them. One point of contact, one invoice, and if anything's wrong with the product it's our problem to sort out, not yours.

Installation only — you already bought them

Ordered from a big-box store or online and now they're sitting in boxes? We'll fit them properly. Bring us the blinds and the windows and we'll do the rest — no judgement about where they came from.

Motorising what you already have

Plenty of existing roller and cellular blinds can take a retrofit motor without replacing the blind itself. Cheaper than starting over, and it gets you the cordless, scheduled version of what's already on the window.

The process

Measure, choose, fit.

01

Free in-home measure

We come to you and measure every window properly. You'll see samples of the fabrics and types in your own light, against your own walls — which matters more than you'd think.

02

Choose and order

We help you narrow it down by room: blackout where you sleep, sheer where you sit, moisture-safe where it's wet. Then we place the order and handle the supplier.

03

Fitted and finished

Usually two to four weeks later we're back to install. Brackets level, blinds square, packaging taken away with us. If they're motorised, we set the schedules before we go.
