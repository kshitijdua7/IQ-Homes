A GTA smart home company, run by someone who picks up the phone.

IQ Homes was built on a simple frustration: smart home technology that's genuinely brilliant, sold and installed in a way that makes people give up on it.

The founder

Kshitij Dua

I started IQ Homes because I kept watching the same thing happen. Someone buys a smart lock, then a doorbell from a different brand, then a couple of bulbs on sale. Six months later there are four apps on their phone, two of them don't talk to each other, and the whole thing has quietly stopped being used.

The technology was never the problem. The problem was that nobody sat in the house, looked at how the family actually lived in it, and designed one system that fit. That's the entire job, and it's what IQ Homes does.

We're based in the GTA and we work across all of it — the City of Toronto and every municipality in Peel, York, Durham and Halton. Small enough that you deal with the same people from the first walkthrough to the call you make eighteen months later. That's deliberate.

Our mission

Make the smart home feel less like technology and more like a house that pays attention.

When it's done right, you stop noticing it. The lights are already how you like them. The door is locked. The house is warm. Nobody in the family had to learn anything, and nobody is opening an app to make it happen.

How we work

Four things we don't compromise on.

We show up ourselves

The person who designs your system installs it and answers the phone afterward. That's not a policy — it's just how a local business this size works, and it's the main reason people choose us over a chain.

We spec what the home needs

There's no package we're pushing. If two rooms of lighting and a decent lock solves your actual problem, we'll tell you that and quote it, rather than talking you into a system you won't use.

We're straight about the trade-offs

Some things are worth automating and some aren't. Some devices don't play well together. We'd rather tell you that at the consultation than have you find out after the invoice.

We build it to grow

Almost nobody does the whole house at once. We design so you can start with one floor and add blinds, cameras or audio in two years without tearing out what's already there.

Local matters

Why a GTA company installs a GTA home better.

Homes here have their own quirks — the housing stock, the winters, the way older Toronto semis are wired versus a new build in Springdale.

A national installer sends whoever is closest with a standard kit. We already know that a 1950s East York bungalow needs a different networking approach than a four-storey Markham new build, and that a January install has to account for a lock that lives at minus twenty. That knowledge only comes from working here.
