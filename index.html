One app. One voice. Every room.

Most “smart homes” are just eleven apps in a trench coat.

A smart bulb here, a doorbell there, a thermostat that lives in its own app and argues with your phone. That's not a smart home — that's homework.

We design the whole thing as one system. Your locks, lights, cameras, climate, blinds and audio all answer to a single app and a single voice assistant — the one you already use. It should feel less like operating technology and more like the house simply paying attention.
